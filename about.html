About Us

"You open Dudi PDF Editor for the first time and notice a clean tool that doesn’t push you to sign up or pay. You type, edit, and save—fast, private, free."

Our Story

In 2026, a 17-year-old named Vikram Dudi built Dudi PDF Editor from his bedroom. He started because he was frustrated with other sites that felt buggy, invasive, or costly. Coming from a middle-class background, he wanted a tool that respects privacy and stays free.

He put months of work into a single website, handling every detail himself. There was no team to rely on, only a steady flow of late nights and early mornings. He built it to be genuinely useful for people who just want a straightforward PDF editor.

Our Mission

We provide a free, privacy-focused PDF editor that anyone can use without hassle. No signups, no paid features, just solid editing tools you can trust.

What We Believe

  • Privacy First: We respect your data. All PDF files are processed securely in real-time and are never stored on any server. Your privacy comes first in every tool we build.
  • A simple, reliable editor serves real people better than gimmicks.
  • A DIY, no-frills approach works best for a free product.
