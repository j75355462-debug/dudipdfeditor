It is important to check a few key settings before converting an Excel sheet to PDF. ### 1. Is the data correct? First, review the entire spreadsheet. Check that: * The numbers are correct. * Formulas are yielding the correct results. * There are no spelling mistakes in the text. * All necessary rows and columns are present. ### 2. Check the Page Layout Check the **Page Layout** section in Excel. Here, you can see how the spreadsheet will appear when printed or saved as a PDF. ### 3. Choose the Orientation If your sheet has many columns, **Landscape** orientation might be more suitable. If the document is small and has fewer columns, **Portrait** orientation might be appropriate. ### 4. Check Margins If page margins are too wide, data might spill over onto the next page. Therefore, adjust the margins as needed. --- # How to Convert Excel Sheets to PDF Free Online? There are several ways to convert an Excel file to PDF.
