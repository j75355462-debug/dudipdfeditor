# How to Rotate PDF Pages and Save Them Permanently – A Complete Guide to Permanently Rotating PDF Pages In today's digital age, PDF documents are widely used for education, office work, business, online applications, and personal documentation. While PDF is a convenient format, pages sometimes do not appear in the correct orientation. Certain pages might appear **sideways**, **upside down**, or in the wrong orientation. For instance, suppose you scanned a document using your mobile phone and accidentally saved some pages in landscape mode. When you open the PDF, some pages appear upright, while others are rotated by 90 or 180 degrees. In such situations, rotating the PDF pages becomes necessary. However, simply rotating a page temporarily within a PDF viewer isn't always enough. If you want the rotated page to open in that same orientation next time, you need to **save the changes permanently**. In this article, we will explore **how to rotate PDF pages and save them permanently**, look at easy methods for rotating pages on mobile and PC, understand the difference between temporary and permanent rotation, and see how **Dudi PDF Editor** can be used for PDF editing. --- ## What Does Rotating a PDF Page Mean? Rotating a PDF page means changing its orientation. Typically, the following rotation options are available for PDFs: * Rotate Clockwise * Rotate Counterclockwise * Rotate 90° * Rotate 180° * Rotate 270° For example, if a page appears sideways, rotating it by 90 degrees can bring it into a normal reading position. If a page is completely upside down, a 180-degree rotation might be required. --- # Difference Between Temporary Rotation and Permanent Rotation It is crucial to understand this distinction. ### Temporary Rotation Many PDF readers feature a "Rotate View" option. This allows the PDF to appear in the correct orientation on the screen temporarily, but it does not necessarily change the actual page orientation within the PDF file itself. When you close and reopen the document, the page might revert to its original orientation. ### Permanent Rotation Permanent rotation saves the actual orientation of the PDF page. This means that even if you open the PDF later on a different device or in another PDF reader, the rotated page will appear in that same orientation. Therefore, if you intend to send the PDF to someone else or use it in the future, **saving it permanently is the better option.** --- # How to Rotate PDF Pages and Save Them Permanently? Let’s look at the step-by-step process for permanently rotating PDF pages. ## Step 1: Open a PDF Editing Tool First, open a PDF editor or page-management tool that allows you to rotate PDF pages and save the changes. If you are using **Dudi PDF Editor**, you can open your PDF in it and manage the page orientation using the available editing features. **Dudi PDF Editor Tip:** If only a few pages in the PDF are in the wrong direction, identify and rotate those specific pages as needed, rather than rotating the entire PDF. --- ## Step 2: Upload the PDF File Now, upload or open your PDF file within the tool. Once the PDF is uploaded, you will be able to see its pages. Many PDF editors display page thumbnails, making it easier to identify pages with the wrong orientation. --- ## Step 3: Select the Page with the Wrong Orientation Select the page you wish to rotate. For instance, if only page 7 and page 15 of your 20-page PDF are sideways, it is best to select only those specific pages. This ensures the orientation of the other pages remains unchanged. --- ## Step 4: Choose the Rotate Option Now, select the Rotate option. Depending on your requirements, you can choose the appropriate option: * 90° Clockwise * 90° Counterclockwise * 180° * 270° If the page is sideways, a 90° rotation is usually sufficient. If the page is upside down, a 180° rotation might be needed. --- ## Step 5: Check Page Orientation Examine the page carefully after rotating it. Check: * Whether the text appears upright. * Whether the images are in the correct orientation. * Whether the entire page content is visible. * Whether the page orientation matches the rest of the document. If the orientation isn't right, you can rotate it in the other direction. --- ## Step 6: Save Changes This is the most important step. Do not close the tool simply after rotating the page. You should use options like **Save**, **Apply Changes**, **Save PDF**, or something similar. This step is essential to permanently save the rotation to the PDF file. After saving, reopen the PDF to verify the changes. --- # How to Rotate PDF Pages on Mobile? Managing PDF documents on smartphones is very common these days. Therefore, knowing how to rotate PDFs on mobile can also be useful. The general process is as follows: 1. Open your mobile browser. 2. Open a PDF editor or PDF rotation tool. 3. Select the PDF file. 4. Open the PDF within the tool. 5. Select the page you want to rotate.
